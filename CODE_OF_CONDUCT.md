# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Neo Brutalism UI project a welcoming, respectful, and positive experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, nationality, identity, or personal characteristics.

We welcome contributions from developers of all skill levels and encourage collaboration, learning, and constructive feedback.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, inclusive, and considerate.
- Providing constructive feedback and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Respecting different perspectives, experiences, and skill levels.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Giving credit where credit is due.
- Helping newcomers feel welcome and supported.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disrespectful comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that is inappropriate or harmful in a collaborative environment.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other interactions related to Neo Brutalism UI.

It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer through the repository's available GitHub contact channels.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate based on the severity and circumstances of a violation, including:

1. A warning.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of contributions or comments.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers will aim to handle reports fairly and proportionately.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), a widely used standard for open-source communities.

## Final Note

Neo Brutalism UI is an open-source project built around sharing, learning, and creating better user interfaces. Everyone who participates is expected to help maintain a respectful and collaborative community.
